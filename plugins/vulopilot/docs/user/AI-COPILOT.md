# AI Copilot

## What it does

AI Copilot is a chat assistant that knows your website. You type a question in plain English, such as "Why is my traffic dropping?", and it answers using your real scan results, not general advice. It can also write a blog post for you and save it as a draft.

## Why it matters

Scan results can be a long list of technical terms. The Copilot explains them in everyday language, tells you what to do first and saves you from searching the internet for answers.

New to VuloPilot? See [GETTING-STARTED](GETTING-STARTED.md).

## Before you start: connect to VuloCloud

AI features need a connection to **VuloCloud**, VuloPilot's AI service. Without it, everything else in VuloPilot still works.

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations → VuloCloud AI**.
2. Click **Connect to VuloCloud**.
3. Check the status:
   - **Connected** - you are ready.
   - **Key needed** - you are connected, but no AI key is set up. Add one in your VuloCloud account, or ask your agency.
   - **Not Connected** - click **Connect** again.

On the Copilot page, **Online** in the header means it can answer; **Offline** means it cannot.

## How to use it

### Ask a question

1. Open **VuloPilot → AI Copilot**.
2. Type in the box ("Ask VuloPilot anything about your website...") or click a suggested prompt, such as **Improve my homepage**, **Why is traffic dropping?**, **Fix my Core Web Vitals**, **Find security issues** or **Make my site GEO ready**.
3. Read the answer. For SEO, speed and security questions, the Copilot gives advice. You make the change, or use the fix buttons elsewhere in VuloPilot.

### Have it write a blog post

Ask "Write a blog about ..." and the Copilot writes it and saves it as a **draft**. It is never published for you, and you can **Undo** it.

### Show it a file or picture

Click **Attach** or **Upload File** to share a text or CSV file, or a picture (JPG, PNG, GIF or WebP). It is saved to your Media Library first so the assistant can read it. If you see "That file wasn't uploaded", use the **Upload File** button.

## You stay in control

- The **Auto-applies** switch lets VuloPilot prepare a fix by itself, but the change still waits for your approval before it goes live.
- Made a change you do not like? Use **Undo**. You will see "Change undone."

## What is on the side of the page

| Panel | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Recent conversations** | Your earlier chats. **View all history** opens the full list |
| **Issues** | Problems from your latest scans, with **More Details** |
| **Recommended by VuloPilot** | The most important suggested actions, with **Investigate with AI** or **Improve with AI** |
| **Site Overview** | Your score and open problems by category, with **View all issues** |

## About AI credits

Some AI actions use **credits**. If you run out, you will be asked to add more. To stop using AI, go to **Settings → Integrations → VuloCloud AI** and click **Disconnect**.
