# Publishing a form

A form must be **Published** before visitors can see or send it. A draft shows nothing to visitors.

Open the form and select the **Share** tab.

## On this WordPress site

**Block editor**

1. Edit the page or post.
2. Add a block and search for **VuloForm**.
3. Choose your form from the list. Only published forms are listed.

**Shortcode**

Copy the shortcode, for example `[vuloform id="12"]`, and paste it into a page, a text widget or a page builder's shortcode element.

## On another website

You can show a form on a site that is not this WordPress site: a landing page, a shop on another platform, a static site.

1. On the **Share** tab, under **On another website**, select **Copy**.
2. Paste the code into the other page's HTML, at the place the form should appear.

The form loads from your WordPress site and submissions arrive here, under **Submissions**, as usual.

Things to know:

- Your WordPress site must stay online; it serves the form.
- The form needs JavaScript on the other site. Visitors without it see a short note instead.
- If the other site uses a strict Content Security Policy, its developer must allow your WordPress site's address.
- Use `https` on both sites.

## Unpublishing

Open the form and select **Unpublish**. Pages that contain it show nothing in its place to visitors. Existing submissions are kept.

## Deleting a form

**Forms > Delete** removes the form together with all its submissions and uploaded files. This cannot be undone, and you are asked to confirm.
