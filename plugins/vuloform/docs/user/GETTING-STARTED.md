# Getting started

## 1. Create a form

1. In the WordPress menu, open **VuloForm**.
2. Select **New form**.
3. Pick a starting point: **Blank form** or one of the ready-made forms (contact, inquiry, lead generation, newsletter signup, feedback, survey, event registration, job application, support request, order or quote inquiry).

The builder opens with your new form. It is a draft: nobody can see it yet.

## 2. Adjust it

- Select a field in the middle of the screen to change its label, make it required or change other settings on the right.
- Drag a field type from the left onto the form to add it, or just click it to add it at the end.
- Type a name for the form at the top.

## 3. Check where notifications go

Open the **Settings** tab of the form. Ready-made forms come with one notification that emails the site's admin address each time the form is submitted. Change the address if someone else should receive it.

## 4. Preview

Select **Preview** to try the form exactly as visitors will see it. Nothing you enter in the preview is sent or saved.

## 5. Publish

Your changes have been saving by themselves as a draft. Select **Publish** to make the form live. If something would stop the form from working, for example a dropdown with no choices, VuloForm tells you what to fix and keeps the form as a draft.

## 6. Put it on a page

Open the **Share** tab.

- **Block editor:** edit a page, add the **VuloForm** block and choose your form.
- **Anywhere else:** copy the shortcode, for example `[vuloform id="12"]`, and paste it where the form should appear.

Open the page, fill in the form and send it. Then look under **VuloForm > Submissions**: your test is there.
