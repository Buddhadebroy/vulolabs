# Reviews

## What it does

**VuloCart → Reviews** is a moderation queue for the ratings and comments customers leave on your products. Nothing a customer writes is shown in your store until you approve it.

The screen exists while the **Product Reviews** module is switched on (Settings → Modules).

## Why it matters

Reviews build trust, but spam and abuse harm it. Moderation lets you keep the good ones.

## How to use it

1. Open **VuloCart → Reviews**. The list shows Product, From (name), Email, Rating, Title, Review, Status and Submitted date.
2. Filter by status: **All**, **Pending**, **Approved** or **Rejected**, or search the reviews.
3. For each review choose **Approve**, **Reject** or **Delete**. You can also select several reviews and **Mark as** a status for all of them, or delete them.
4. **Export** downloads the list.

A review from someone who did not give a name is shown as "Anonymous". A review of a product that was deleted shows "(deleted product)".

The Dashboard counts the reviews waiting for you and links here ("Moderate").

## Switching the module off

If you switch Product Reviews off, the Reviews screen and the review routes disappear. **Your stored reviews are kept** and come back when you switch it on again.
