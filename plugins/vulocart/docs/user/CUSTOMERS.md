# Customers

## What it does

**Customers** keeps a record of everyone who has placed an order: their contact details, saved addresses, order history and your private notes. A record is created automatically the first time someone orders, matched by email.

## Why it matters

You see who your best customers are, who buys again, and who has gone quiet. Notes let your team share what they know.

## The customer list

**Customers → All Customers** shows Customer (name and "Guest" or "Registered"), Email, Phone, Orders, Total spent, Last order and Since.

Ready-made views along the top:

| View | Shows |
|---|---|
| All | Every customer |
| New | Joined in the last 30 days |
| Repeat buyers | Two or more orders |
| High value | Spent 500 or more |
| Inactive | Ordered before, but not in 90 days |
| No orders | Have never placed an order |
| Registered | Have a site account |
| Guests | Checked out without an account |

You can **search name or email**, **Save view** to keep the current view and search as your own tab, **Quick edit** a customer's details, **Export** the list, and select several customers to delete them.

## A customer's page

Click a customer to open their page.

- **Orders**, **Total spent**, **Lifetime value**, **Average order** and **Last order** at the top.
- **Order history** - every order with its payment and fulfillment status.
- **Profile** - email, name and phone.
- **Addresses** - saved billing and shipping addresses. Add, edit or delete them; mark one as **Default billing** or **Default shipping**. A signed-in customer can also manage their own address book.
- **Notes** - internal only. Customers never see them.

Press **Update** to save changes; the page warns you when you have unsaved changes.

## Deleting a customer

Deleting a customer removes their profile, saved addresses and notes. **Their orders are kept**, because they are your sales records and carry the customer's name and email.

## Pro

With VuloCart Pro and the *Customer Growth* module, the customer page also has wishlist, saved carts, groups, segments, loyalty points, credit and wallet balance, communication history and a merged timeline.
