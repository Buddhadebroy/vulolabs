# Dashboard

**VuloCart → Dashboard** is the first screen you see. It shows how your store is doing and what needs your attention today.

## What it does

The Dashboard reads your orders, customers and stock and turns them into figures, a chart and short to-do lists. Nothing here changes data; every item links to the screen where you can act.

## Why it matters

You see problems early: unpaid orders, orders waiting to be fulfilled, products that are almost out of stock, reviews waiting for approval.

## How to use it

1. Choose a **date range** at the top: **7 days**, **30 days** or **90 days**. The choice is remembered in your browser.
2. Read the **Key figures**: Revenue, Orders, Average order and New customers. Each shows the change against the previous period of the same length ("vs previous 30 days").
3. Look at the **Sales** chart. Use the **chart metric** switch to see revenue or orders per day. The chart also shows your average per day and your best day.
4. Check **What needs your attention**. Each line has a button that takes you to the fix:
   - **Restock** - products that are out of stock
   - **Fulfil** - paid orders that are not yet fulfilled
   - **Review** - orders that are still awaiting payment
   - **Reorder** - products that are running low
   - **Moderate** - product reviews waiting for approval

   When there is nothing to do the panel says "You're all caught up" and offers **Create a coupon** (a Pro feature).
5. **Recent orders** lists the latest orders with their payment and fulfillment status. Press **All orders** for the full list.
6. **Top products** shows your best sellers and how much of your revenue they bring.
7. **Stock health** shows how many products are healthy, low or out of stock, and lists the ones that need action.

The header buttons **Add product**, **Create order** and **View store** are shortcuts.

### First-time checklist

Before your first order the Dashboard shows "Get ready for your first order" with three steps: **Add products**, **Set up payments** and **Place a test order**.

### Store insights (Pro)

With VuloCart Pro and the Analytics module switched on, a **Store insights** section appears with conversion, lifetime value, retention, the checkout funnel, abandoned carts, top customers, AI insights and stock forecasts. Without it the section is not shown.

## Settings that affect it

| Setting | Where | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Store currency | Settings → General | Currency used for the figures |
| Low stock threshold | Settings → General | A product at or below it counts as "low" |
